WHY NOW

The BPO inflection point

Afni's economics are driven by the cost and quality of high-volume voice interactions and the cost and speed of hiring the people who handle them. This proposal targets both — before AI-native competitors reprice the market.

Margin pressureAI-native providers reset price and quality expectations.
Attrition & rampHigh turnover keeps hiring and training in permanent motion.
CX expectationsClients want 24/7, instant, consistent, compliant service.
Move firstBecome an AI-enabled partner — or be repriced.
“Give Afni one secure, governed platform to build, evaluate, deploy, govern and continuously improve fleets of cooperating AI agents — with the same operational rigor Afni already applies to running contact centers.

FLAGSHIP USE CASES

Two use cases, one platform

Sequenced from lowest to highest risk, each delivering measurable value early.

USE CASE 1 · VOICE AI

Voice AI for contact centers

  • Agent-assist copilot — live transcription, next-best-action, compliance nudges, auto-summary
  • Autonomous voice agent — sub-second speech-to-speech for containable call types
  • Post-call analytics & QA — 100% coverage, not sampled
KPITarget *
Containment20–40% of eligible calls
AHT15–25% reduction
QA coverage~5% → 100%
USE CASE 2 · HR RECRUITMENT

AI-driven HR recruitment

  • Agents per stage — JD generation, screening, conversational pre-screen, scheduling, scoring assist
  • AI assists, humans decide — no autonomous rejection
  • Fairness built in — EEOC, NYC LL144 bias audits, IL AI Video Interview Act, EU AI Act, GDPR
