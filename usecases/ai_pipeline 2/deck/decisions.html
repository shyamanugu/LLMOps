Section 03

Decisions we need from you

The platform and the APIX proof point are done. These are the real, concrete choices that shape the next milestone — cost, scope, timeline, and go-live. Each one changes what we build next.

Answer drives scopeNo wrong answers — just trade-offs

A · Go-live & environment

1Azure landing zone

Which subscription, resource group, and region do we target? What access do we have — Contributor at RG level, or can roles be assigned? Why it matters: decides how we provision and whether we use key-auth or managed identity.

2Environments

Do we need dev / test / prod separated now, or one environment to start? Why it matters: model aliases, guardrail policy, and eval thresholds are all per-environment.

B · Models & cost

3Approved model list & pricing

Which Azure OpenAI deployments are approved for reason (analysis) and bulk (denoise)? What are the per-token rates? Why it matters: cost tracking shows $0 until real rates are in; a cheaper bulk model can cut denoise spend materially.

4Cost & FinOps guardrails

Do we need budget alerts / spend caps, and at what thresholds? Why it matters: turns on component 12 (Azure budgets) and dashboards.

C · Data, privacy & safety

5PII policy on transcripts

Is flag-for-audit acceptable, or must we redact/mask PII in outputs? Any category (SSN, card) that must hard-block? Why it matters: one policy file — but it changes what agents see and what we store.

6Content Safety & residency

Do we enable Azure AI Content Safety, and are there data-residency constraints (region, retention)? Why it matters: enables component 06 cloud moderation and shapes storage.

D · Operations & ownership

7Golden-dataset ownership

Who curates ground truth, and how many cases can we get per program to start? Why it matters: the eval gate is only as strong as the golden set behind it.

8Human-in-the-loop

Do coaches review/approve AI reflections before they reach agents? Why it matters: decides whether the feedback tab is advisory or an approval gate.

E · Deployment & next milestone

9Deployment shape

Batch job on a schedule (Container Apps Job, scale-to-zero) vs. an always-on service? What cadence — nightly, weekly? Why it matters: sets the hosting pattern and cost profile.

10Next use case to onboard

Which second use case should we bring on to prove reuse — Voice Agent, PI Index, Hiring, or something else? Why it matters: this is the strongest demonstration of ROI.

Recommended first moves (our view)

1) Provide the approved models + rates (unlocks real cost).   2) Confirm the PII policy.   3) Point us at a small golden set for APIX.   4) Pick the second use case. With those four, we can flip APIX to real and start the next onboarding in parallel.
