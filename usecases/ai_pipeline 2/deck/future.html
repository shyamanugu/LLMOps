Section 04

Build once, reuse everywhere

APIX proved the pattern. The same platform — same guardrails, same observability, same eval gate, same console — powers the next use case as a configuration exercise, not a rebuild. Time-to-value drops from quarters to weeks.

The only new artefacts are config and a golden dataset. Everything else is inherited from the platform.

The three flagship use cases the platform is built for

An explainable performance score from 100% of interactions. Reuses the exact analysis/summary pattern APIX already uses — largely config + prompts.

Decisioning

Why this matters to AFNI

Risk and cost are controlled by construction: every use case is observable, evaluated, and governed the same way. The 4th, 10th, and 40th use case ride the same paved road — that is the entire thesis of the platform, and APIX is the proof it works.
